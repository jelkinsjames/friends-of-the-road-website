Friends of the Road

Discography & canon

Show posters · Discography & canon · Press & radio

Records by Friends of the Road, plus the wider circle of releases by band members and collaborators. Around here, the latter is the “canon.”

Release information is carried over from the Friends of the Road canon on Shonepine. The related releases below are credited to their own artists, not presented as band albums.

Friends of the Road releases

  • split — Bicentennial Memorial Accordion Choir / Friends of the Road

    Released May 1, 2026. Two extended drone improvisations.

  • split — Free Water Shrew Ensemble / Friends of the Road

    Released November 14, 2025. Two long pieces recorded in Portland and Seattle in June 2025.

  • Live — Friends of the Road

    Released June 19, 2025. Original pieces and traditional tunes recorded on the road in June and July 2023.

  • Sunseekin' Blues — Friends of the Road

    Released July 19, 2024. Six tracks bringing together original pieces and traditional tunes.

  • Now You Know Something Right Here And I'll Tell You For A Fact — Friends of the Road

    Released January 26, 2023. A ten-track collection.

The wider canon: members & collaborations

  • Kennedy Webb & Friends — Kennedy Webb

    Released October 9, 2026. Eight tracks for fiddle, banjo, voice, and ensemble, including “Quail Eggs” with Friends of the Road.

  • the how to sing tape — Elli Ott

    Released July 21, 2026. Nine pieces for guitar, voice, cello, and electronics.

  • Clark / Siskin — Kelby Clark & Sadie Siskin

    Released April 24, 2026. A split tape of two live performances recorded in 2025: Kelby Clark’s “Puriya Dhanashree Blues” and Sadie Siskin’s “Sugar Baby Sonata.”

  • Sadie Siskin — Sadie Siskin

    Released May 23, 2025. Solo banjo improvisations rooted in old-time music, alongside organ, voice, and layered bowed drones. Recorded in Seattle in 2025.

  • Little Maggie — Sadie Siskin

    Released August 24, 2024. Two solo banjo improvisations on the traditional tune “Little Maggie,” released by Searching Records.
